Workshop Overview

Workshop Objective

In 2 hours you will understand the USB attack chain — device emulation, privilege concepts, remote access paths — and how defenders detect and block it. Educational & defensive use only.

How a USB Device Can Control a Computer

Learn how a USB device can emulate a keyboard and send commands. Understand what programmable USB devices are and why defenders must treat them as a real risk.

Windows Security & Privilege Concepts

Learn how Windows protects computers, what administrator privileges mean, how UAC and related controls work, and where automation-based risks appear.

Remote Access & Networking

Understand Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP), basic networking concepts, NAT, firewalls, and tunneling — and the security implications of each.

Complete Architecture Understanding

See how USB device → Windows → RDP → tunnel → remote client fit together as one attack chain — and where defenders can break that chain.

Curriculum

What You'll Learn

Everything explained in simple language so anyone can understand. Learn how the attacks work, why they work, and how to stop them. Purely educational and ethical learning.

Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking Basics

What cybersecurity is, what ethical hacking means, legal and illegal hacking, what you can and cannot do, and why understanding these techniques is important.

How USB Devices Work

How your computer recognizes a USB, how a USB can pretend to be a keyboard, what makes a USB dangerous, and why most people don't know about this.

BadUSB and Automated Attacks

What happens when a USB device is misused for automation, how command injection via HID works in principle, and what the real dangers are for users and organizations.

Complete Attack Chain Understanding

From USB to privilege concepts to remote access paths. Understand how each stage connects — and where defenders can interrupt the chain.

Windows Privileges & Security Controls

Why admin privileges matter, what security features Windows provides, and how defenders monitor and harden these areas.

Remote Desktop Concepts

What Remote Desktop is, how remote access works in principle, and the security implications of exposing remote services.

Command Sequences & Safe Analysis

How to read and understand the kinds of commands a malicious USB might send, and how to study them safely in a lab without harming production systems.

How to Protect Yourself

How to stop these attacks, what signs to look for, how to protect your computer, and what organizations can do to stay safe.

Workshop Agenda

2-Hour Breakdown

Comprehensive 15-module curriculum. Every minute is structured for complete architecture understanding.

0–10 min

Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking Foundations

Cybersecurity basics, penetration testing, authorized vs unauthorized testing, attacker/defender perspectives, responsible use, and legal boundaries.

10–25 min

USB Device Fundamentals

How USB devices communicate, device identification, keyboard-style interaction, programmable vs normal USB devices.

25–40 min

Understanding Bad USB & USB Rubber Ducky

BadUSB concept, how devices behave differently, USB automation applications, cybersecurity uses, risks, and defensive strategies.

40–55 min

Our Custom Technique & Architecture Overview

Introduction to the custom technique, high-level architecture, how USB, Windows, RDP, and networking connect together conceptually.

55–75 min

Understanding the Payload & Windows Fundamentals

Payload breakdown, Windows accounts, permissions, UAC, services, firewall, local groups, Remote Desktop configuration, environment variables.

75–90 min

Remote Desktop Protocol Fundamentals

How RDP works, client vs host, RDP service, permissions, firewall considerations, port 3389, and security implications.

90–100 min

Networking Fundamentals

IP addresses, private vs public networks, ports, TCP, client/server model, NAT, firewalls, and reachability concepts.

100–110 min

Tunneling & Remote Connectivity

Network tunnels, reverse tunnels, connecting local services through external intermediaries, architecture fit, and security risks.

110–117 min

Complete Architecture & Payload Analysis

Putting everything together. Analyzing payloads, understanding dependencies, identifying stages, and safe modification techniques in labs.

117–120 min

Defender Perspective & Q&A

Detecting USB activity, monitoring account/RDP/service/firewall changes, detecting tunnels, defensive strategies, and open questions.

Ethics & Legal

Strictly Educational & Ethical

PakSec Nation operates under a strict ethical code. This workshop is designed to educate — not enable harm. Every participant must agree to our ethics policy before joining.

Educational Purpose Only

All content is strictly conceptual and theoretical. No real attack tools, live payloads, or working exploit code will be shared or demonstrated during this workshop.

Consent & Ethics Agreement

Every participant must sign a digital consent form acknowledging that the knowledge gained is for defensive, educational, and research purposes only — never for unauthorized access.

Responsible Disclosure Culture

We teach the responsible disclosure mindset — if you discover a vulnerability, you report it ethically to the affected organization, not exploit it for personal gain.

No Unauthorized Use

Applying any technique taught in this workshop against systems, networks, or devices you do not own or have explicit written permission to test is strictly prohibited and illegal.

PECA 2016 (as amended) — Know the Law

Under Pakistan's Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (as amended), unauthorized access to computer systems, networks, or data is a criminal offence punishable by imprisonment and fines. The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) investigates these offences. This workshop exists to help you understand threats so you can defend against them — not to enable illegal activity. Any misuse is solely the responsibility of the individual.
